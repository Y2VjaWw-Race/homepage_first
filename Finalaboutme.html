My brother, sturdy and broad, lives another day with no future in mind. He dances all day and all night, a vigor unappealed. From gigantic shakes to the gentlest of sways he never sits still always moving in ridiculous ways even on the cloudiest of days in rain in storm in the night time fog the moving never stops brother likes it this way closer to Mother who cares for the single wheat across the street. She who holds all natural life has never disrespected is slowly changing from disrespect the bugs have stopped crawling about in, under, and over me the turn of the air become sour, like the stagnace of a cave unlike turned over dirt it is a putrid stench maggots will die in Brother eats minerals in the most beautiful way. How they are grinded down to specks of stardust, they glisten like the sky above.
