Publication

Love and Destruction

The pamphlete in the order of a 16 page one paper. It is made with the idea of a modern information Brochure, limited to the construction of one larger paper instead of a classic zine. With the backside to a pamphlete I created involving the process of creating marks and deconstructing the sponge. This plays on the rectangular strcture of the original object and elements of Japanese Graphics (in both the design and language). The use of repetition and purposefully inconsistent squares presents a slow destruction of the sponge throughout time and leads the eye through the poster.
